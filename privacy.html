VAREON STUDIO OFFICIAL ("we", "our", or "us") built the PP QUEST Android application (com.ppm.quest) and accompanying web portal (https://ppmod.netlify.app). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle user information when you use our application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide core app functionality, account management, and order processing, we may collect the following minimal information:

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, unique user identifier (UID), and optional linked Telegram ID/username provided when you sign in.
  • In-App Activity & Wallet Records: Service order history, reward points, coin balance, and transaction logs required to deliver requested gaming services and support inquiries.
  • Device & Security Identifiers: An anonymous app-scoped device identifier used strictly for push notifications, account security, fraud prevention, and app integrity verification.
  • Temporary One-Time Session Data: When you explicitly initiate a one-time Google Play Games quest action, authentication is handled strictly for that single requested action and is not stored permanently on our servers.

2. Android Permissions We Use

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to communicate securely with our cloud servers and load live game services and balance updates.
  • GET_ACCOUNTS: Used with Android's native system account picker so you can conveniently select an existing Google account on your device to sign in.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS & VIBRATE: Used to notify you about completed orders, balance updates, and important service announcements.
  • REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES: Used strictly when you tap the in-app "Update Now" button to download and install an official verified update of PP QUEST.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account and synchronize your balance and order history accurately.
  • Process requested game quest and profile services and provide verifiable order status updates.
  • Protect the app and users from unauthorized tampering, abuse, or fraudulent activity.
  • Provide customer support and deliver critical maintenance or update notices.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data or passwords to any third party. We rely on trusted infrastructure providers solely to operate the service:

  • Cloudflare (Workers, D1 & R2): Secure API routing, encrypted database storage, and official app update delivery.
  • Google Services: Used solely for user-initiated sign-in and Google Play Games profile/service operations.
  • Telegram: Optional notification delivery and customer support when linked by the user.

5. Data Security & Retention

All network communications between PP QUEST and our servers are encrypted over HTTPS/TLS. We never ask for or store your Google password in our database. Order and transaction logs are retained only as long as necessary to maintain your account history and resolve support requests.

6. Children's Privacy

PP QUEST is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

7. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your PP QUEST account and associated cloud data at any time by contacting our official support channel below with your registered email address or UID. Valid deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.
